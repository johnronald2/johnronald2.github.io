How CampusCare Solves Problems

From the moment you notice an issue on campus to its verified resolution, here is how our structured redressal cycle works.

01

Report & Tag

Submit your grievance with live camera capture, document PDF, or scan the room’s QR tag to auto-detect location.

QR & Live Cam proof
02

Auto-Route

CampusCare intelligently assigns the ticket to the exact department nodal head (IT, Estate, Exams, Hostel, or Canteen).

Nodal department alert
03

Community Poll

If other students face the same issue, they vote and support it. Multiple votes automatically boost priority without duplicates.

Community upvoting
04

Action & SLA

Department officers inspect, repair, and upload resolution notes. If delayed, tickets auto-escalate to the Dean.

Live progress timeline
05

Rate & Rewards

Review the fix and give 1-5 star feedback. Students earn campus karma points and badges for civic engagement!

Badges & Karma points

Key Features Designed for Speed & Integrity

Every detail is tailored to eliminate administrative friction and give students, faculty, and leadership crystal-clear transparency.

Campus QR Location Scanner

Scan QR tags posted on lab doors, classroom podiums, or hostel blocks using your smartphone camera to auto-fill exact coordinates.

Zero typing required for room details

Live Cam & Document Upload

Snap live photos of broken benches, projector glitches, or attach fee receipts, medical certificates, and PDF documents with instant preview.

Direct webcam & gallery file support

Community Duplicate Polls

Detects existing tickets for shared issues (Wi-Fi, water, canteen). Students upvote the main thread instead of submitting 50 redundant complaints.

Votes boost SLA urgency directly

Whistleblower Confidential Shield

Zero-retaliation institutional policy

Distinguished faculty tags on Admin panel
